# FAQ

<Lead>Short answers to what developers ask first.</Lead>

## What kind of apps can I deploy?

Any app with a `Dockerfile`, in any language. Without one, Foyr detects Next.js. Node and Python detection are next.

## Do I need to change my code to get login?

No. The proxy handles it before requests reach your app. The SDK is optional; use it when your app needs to know who is asking.

## Who can see my app?

Only people you invite, unless you make it public.

## Do the people I invite need an account?

No. They sign in with a link sent to their email. No password, no GitHub account.

## Where is my app hosted?

On AWS in Mumbai, `ap-south-1`.

## Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Add it under **Domains**, create the DNS records Foyr shows you, and TLS is set up automatically.

## Can I bring my own database?

Yes. Set its connection string as an environment variable. Managed Postgres for each app is planned.

## How much will it cost?

Free during early access. Simple pricing at launch. See [Pricing](/pricing).
